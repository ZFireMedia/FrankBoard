## Benefit Sections

### 1. Simple
**Keeps boards simple.**

Boards, tasks, columns, swimlanes. No custom fields or workflow engines. If you need a work board that does one thing well, FrankBoard does that.

### 2. Modern UI
**Polished, not plain.**

Typography, spacing, light and dark themes. FrankBoard gives you a cleaner board and task experience than stock Kanboard — without changing how you work.

### 3. Self-hosted
**Your data, your control.**

Docker and PostgreSQL. Deploy on your VPS. No vendor lock-in. Your data stays where you put it.

### 4. Compatible
**Built on Kanboard.**

Same database schema, plugin API, and migration paths. Move in — or back — with a backup. We preserve what Kanboard got right.
