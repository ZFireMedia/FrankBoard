## Intro Copy

**Choose the edition that fits your team.**

FrankBoard offers three ways to run: Community (free, self-hosted), Pro (paid, self-hosted, with support), and Cloud (managed by us). All editions share the same core features. The difference is how you run it and what support you get.

---

## Short Edition Descriptions

### Community
For individuals, small teams, and evaluation. Free, full-featured, self-hosted. No time limit, no feature gates.

### Pro
For teams with commercial use or formal support needs. Same product as Community, plus commercial license and priority support. You still self-host.

### Cloud
For teams that prefer zero hosting. We run FrankBoard for you. Backups included. No Docker, no VPS.

## FAQ

**Can I move from Community to Pro?** Yes. Same deployment, same data. You add a Pro license and support. No migration required.

**What’s included in Cloud?** Managed hosting, backups, upgrades, and support. You sign up; we run it.

**Are core features different between editions?** No. Boards, tasks, swimlanes, and plugins work the same. Pro and Cloud add license, support, and (for Cloud) hosting.

**Can I try before paying?** Community is the trial. Run it free. When you need Pro or Cloud, contact us.
