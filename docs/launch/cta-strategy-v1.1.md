# FrankBoard CTA Strategy v1.1

**Date**: 2025-03-16  
**Scope**: CTA refinement — prioritize migration and setup signal over generic free acquisition.

---

## Primary CTA: Migrate from Kanboard

**Placement**: Hero (or prominent), mid-page, footer  
**Destination**: /migrate/  
**Goal**: Capture Kanboard users with clear migration intent. Highest conversion potential.

**Why this first**:
- Kanboard users are the most qualified audience — they already use the product we're modernizing.
- Migration interest = strong intent. They're evaluating a real switch.
- Migrate page explains compatibility and builds trust.
- Aligns with early validation: "Do people want to move from Kanboard to FrankBoard?"

---

## Secondary CTA: Request Setup Help

**Placement**: Migrate page, support page, pricing (services section)  
**Destination**: mailto:support@frankboard.com (with context: setup, migration, deployment)  
**Goal**: Capture teams that want assistance — deployment, migration, VPS setup. Service revenue signal.

**Why second**:
- Setup help = paid-intent. They're willing to pay for expertise.
- Validates demand for Pro/Cloud services (migration assistance, deployment setup).
- Lower volume than migration, but higher value per lead.

---

## Tertiary CTA: Get Community Free

**Placement**: Nav, footer, migrate page (as alternative path)  
**Destination**: https://github.com/ZFireMedia/FrankBoard  
**Goal**: Self-serve option for visitors who want to try without migrating or requesting help.

**Why third**:
- Still important. Many will discover via migration/setup and then self-serve.
- Keep visible and unambiguous — but don't lead with it in early soft launch.
- GitHub remains canonical distribution; stars and clones are signal.

---

## Messaging Notes

- **Migrate**: "Migrate from Kanboard" or "Moving from Kanboard? Start here."
- **Setup help**: "Request setup help" or "Need migration assistance?" — links to migrate page CTA or mailto.
- **Get Community Free**: Keep copy as-is. Available but not primary in hero/messaging for Phase 1.
