# FrankBoard Soft Launch Strategy v1

**Date**: 2025-03-16  
**Status**: Active  
**Scope**: First controlled public exposure and early demand validation.

---

## Objective

**Primary**: Learn who cares, who converts, and what they need — with minimal spend and founder-level effort.

**Goals**:
1. **Signal** — Does anyone want a modernized Kanboard?
2. **Qualify** — Who are they? (small teams, Kanboard users, ops leads)
3. **Validate** — Do GitHub stars, issues, and contact inquiries indicate real interest?
4. **Iterate** — Use feedback to refine messaging and roadmap.

This is **not** a broad-scale promotion. No paid ads, no PR push. Founder-led outreach to relevant communities and early adopters.

---

## What We Measure

| Metric | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| GitHub stars / clones | Interest signal |
| GitHub issues / discussions | Engagement, feature requests |
| Site traffic (if tracked) | Funnel top |
| Contact form / mailto responses | Qualified leads, Pro/Cloud interest |
| Conversions to "Get Community" (GitHub) | Primary CTA success |

---

## Constraints

- **Founder-speed** — One person can execute. No agency, no campaign team.
- **Low budget** — Zero or near-zero ad spend.
- **Reversible** — If soft launch shows no signal, we learn. No commitment to scale.
- **No enterprise** — Don't promise SSO, SLAs, or features we don't have.

---

## Phasing

| Phase | Focus | Duration |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Phase 1 (now)** | Soft launch pack complete; first outreach; baseline metrics | 2–4 weeks |
| **Phase 2** | Review signal; adjust messaging; expand or narrow channels | After Phase 1 |
| **Phase 3** | Scale what works; Pro/Cloud readiness when demand justifies | TBD |

---

## Success Criteria (Phase 1)

- At least one meaningful engagement (GitHub star, issue, or contact) from non-founders
- No negative launch incidents (site down, broken links, embarrassing copy)
- Clear record of what was tried and what resulted (for Phase 2 decisions)
