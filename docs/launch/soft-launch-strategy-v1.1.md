# FrankBoard Soft Launch Strategy v1.1

**Date**: 2025-03-16  
**Status**: Active (replaces v1 for CTA/channel decisions)  
**Scope**: Refinement — optimize for migration and setup signal over generic free-user acquisition.

---

## Objective

**Primary**: Learn who cares, who converts, and what they need — with minimal spend and founder-level effort.

**Goals**:
1. **Signal** — Does anyone want a modernized Kanboard?
2. **Qualify** — Who are they? (Kanboard users migrating, teams needing setup help, ops leads)
3. **Validate** — Migration interest, setup requests, contact inquiries indicate real intent.
4. **Iterate** — Use feedback to refine messaging and roadmap.

**Refinement (v1.1)**: Early soft launch optimizes for **higher-intent signal** — migration and setup interest — over generic free-user acquisition. Get Community Free remains available but is not the primary CTA.

---

## What We Measure

| Metric | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| Migration / migrate page engagement | High-intent signal |
| Contact (setup help, migration assistance) | Qualified leads, service interest |
| GitHub stars / clones | Interest signal |
| GitHub issues / discussions | Engagement, feature requests |
| Conversions to "Migrate" and "Request setup" | Primary CTA success |

---

## Constraints

- **Founder-speed** — One person can execute. No agency, no campaign team.
- **Low budget** — Zero or near-zero ad spend.
- **Reversible** — If soft launch shows no signal, we learn. No commitment to scale.
- **No enterprise** — Don't promise SSO, SLAs, or features we don't have.

---

## Phasing

| Phase | Focus | Duration |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Phase 1 (now)** | Soft launch pack v1.1; first outreach; baseline metrics | 2–4 weeks |
| **Phase 2** | Review signal; adjust messaging; expand or narrow channels | After Phase 1 |
| **Phase 3** | Scale what works; Pro/Cloud readiness when demand justifies | TBD |

---

## Success Criteria (Phase 1)

- At least one meaningful engagement (migration interest, setup request, or contact) from non-founders
- No negative launch incidents (site down, broken links, embarrassing copy)
- Clear record of what was tried and what resulted (for Phase 2 decisions)
