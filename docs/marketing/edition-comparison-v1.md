# FrankBoard Edition Comparison Page v1

**Date**: 2025-03-15  
**Status**: Strategy document  
**Scope**: Structure for edition comparison page; documentation only, no implementation.

---

## Page Purpose

Help visitors choose the right edition (Community, Pro, Cloud) by presenting a clear, factual comparison. Reduce confusion and support sales conversations for Pro and Cloud.

---

## Recommended Edition Names

| Edition | Name | Rationale |
|---------|------|-----------|
| Free self-hosted | **Community** | Familiar; signals open, no cost |
| Paid self-hosted | **Pro** | Common industry term; implies support and commercial use |
| Managed SaaS | **Cloud** | Clear; implies hosting included |

Avoid: "Starter," "Enterprise," "Premium" (overused or misleading for scope).

## Key Differentiators

| Edition | Emphasize |
|---------|-----------|
| **Community** | Free, full features, self-hosted, no lock-in, plugin ecosystem |
| **Pro** | Commercial license, support when you need it, same product as Community |
| **Cloud** | Zero hosting, backups included, we run it for you |

---

## What to Emphasize for Each Edition

### Community

- **Who it's for**: Individuals, small teams, evaluation, non-commercial use
- **Why choose**: Free, no limits, full control
- **CTA**: "Get Community" (download, Docker, GitHub)

### Pro

- **Who it's for**: Teams with commercial use, need for support, formal licensing
- **Why choose**: Same product + commercial rights + support
- **CTA**: "Contact for Pro" or "Get Pro"

### Cloud

- **Who it's for**: Teams that prefer no hosting, want backups and uptime managed
- **Why choose**: No Docker, no VPS, we handle infra
- **CTA**: "Start Cloud trial" or "Get Cloud"

---

## Language Guidelines

- **Feature parity**: "Same core features across all editions"
- **No gating**: "Core boards and tasks are not paywalled"
- **Support difference**: "Community = best-effort; Pro/Cloud = priority support"
- **Avoid**: "Premium features," "Enterprise grade," "Unlimited"
