# FrankBoard — Pricing Page Structure v1

**Date**: 2025-03-15  
**Status**: Strategy document  
**Scope**: Pricing page purpose, structure, FAQ — no final pricing numbers.

---

## Pricing Page Purpose

- Present the three editions (Community, Pro, Cloud) with clear value propositions.
- Help visitors choose the right fit: free self-hosted, paid self-hosted, or managed.
- Set expectations for support and commercial use without overcommitting.
- Capture interest (contact, waitlist) for Pro and Cloud before they're fully available.

---

## Pricing Model Recommendation

| Edition | Model | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| **Community** | Free | Always free; self-hosted |
| **Pro** | Annual or perpetual + maintenance | Flat or tiered by team size; no per-seat monthly |
| **Cloud** | Per-seat monthly or tiered | E.g., 5 seats, 10 seats; consider small free tier for trial |

See [revenue-model-v1.md](../business/revenue-model-v1.md) for full pricing-shape guidance.

---

## Page Structure

1. **Header**: "Simple pricing. No surprises."
2. **Community**: Free, self-hosted, full features. CTA: "Get Community"
3. **Pro**: Commercial license + support. CTA: "Contact us" or "Coming soon"
4. **Cloud**: Managed hosting. CTA: "Contact us" or "Coming soon"
5. **Edition summary table**: Side-by-side comparison (link to edition-comparison page)
6. **FAQ**: Top 5–7 questions
7. **Bottom CTA**: "Start with Community" or "Questions? Get in touch"

---

## FAQ Topics (Initial)

1. **Is Community really free?** Yes. All core features. Self-hosted. No time limit.
2. **What does Pro include?** Commercial use license, priority support, assurance. Same features as Community.
3. **When will Pro/Cloud be available?** "We're building capacity. Contact us to join the waitlist."
4. **Can I migrate from Kanboard?** Yes. Same data; backup and config swap. Reversible.
5. **Do you offer trials?** Community is the trial. Pro/Cloud trials when available.
6. **What happens if I outgrow Community?** Upgrade to Pro for commercial use and support. No feature lock-in.
7. **Is there a free Cloud tier?** Possibly in the future (1–2 users); not committed yet.

---

## What Should Appear on Pricing Page Initially

- Clear labels: Community, Pro, Cloud.
- Honest status: "Community available now" / "Pro and Cloud coming soon" if not live.
- Value propositions per edition.
- Contact / waitlist mechanism for Pro and Cloud.
- FAQ addressing common concerns.
- Link to edition comparison for detail.

---

## What Should NOT Appear Initially

- **Final pricing numbers** — until Pro/Cloud are defined and approved.
- **Feature gates** — don't imply Pro/Cloud have features Community lacks (except support, hosting, license).
- **Countdown timers or urgency** — no "limited time" or fake scarcity.
- **Unbuilt features** — don't promise automation, AI, or integrations not yet delivered.
- **Enterprise tiers** — avoid "Contact sales" enterprise language until we have capacity.
